# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers of hermes-workflows pledge to make participation in our project a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members

Harassment, abuse, and unwelcoming behavior are not tolerated in any form. This includes but is not limited to: offensive comments related to personal characteristics, deliberate intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, and sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, including the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, and any other official communication channels. It also applies when an individual is representing the project or its community in public spaces.
